01 / ABOUT

I build reliable AI for high-stakes, real-world decision making.

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Prof. Amir M. Rahmani. My research spans generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal learning, causal inference, and privacy-aware biosignal modeling.

Across academia and industry, I have worked on systems that connect LLMs with structured knowledge, causal graphs, multimodal clinical data, and verification pipelines—aiming to make AI outputs more grounded, interpretable, and useful in healthcare.

My research question is not only whether an AI system can produce an answer, but whether it can produce an answer that is grounded, causally coherent, privacy-aware, and useful.
